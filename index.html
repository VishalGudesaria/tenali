THE FOUNDATIONS

Three laws. One universe of motion.

Sir Isaac Newton described how forces affect the motion of objects.

01

Law of Inertia

An object stays at rest, or keeps moving at constant velocity, unless acted on by a net external force.

🚗 Example: Passengers lean forward when a moving car brakes.
02

Law of Acceleration

Acceleration depends on the net force and the object's mass. More force means more acceleration; more mass means less acceleration.

F = m × a
⚽ Example: A harder kick accelerates a ball more.
03

Action & Reaction

For every action force, there is an equal and opposite reaction force. The forces act on different objects.

🚀 Example: A rocket pushes gas downward; gas pushes the rocket upward.

IN THE REAL WORLD
